I finally uploaded the MIDI folder. Forty-two files, sorted by category: games, movies, and "other".

The collection started when a friend gave me a floppy with the DOOM theme and a file called `WONDER.MID` that I have never identified. It might be from a shareware CD. It might be from a dream.

Downloading them took most of a Saturday. I used a download manager that let me pause and resume, which was necessary because my mom picked up the phone twice.

My favorites:

- the DOOM theme, obviously
- the Winamp demo song
- a jazz trio version of the Tetris theme
- a file called `DIALUP.MID` that is just modem noises with a drum track

Please do not hotlink the files. Right-click and save them like a normal person.

If you have MIDI requests, sign the guestbook. I will consider them.
