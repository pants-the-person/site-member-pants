Last night I changed the front side bus jumper from 33 MHz to 40 MHz. The 486 DX2-66 is now running at 80 MHz. It booted on the first try, which is suspicious.

The CPU is warm. Not "egg on the heatsink" warm, but warmer than I would like. I zip-tied a 40mm case fan to the heatsink with electrical tape and it dropped the temperature by about six degrees. The tape is holding. For now.

Windows 98 is stable. DOOM is stable. The only problem is the sound card: at 80 MHz the Sound Blaster 16 sometimes clicks when a MIDI starts.

Things I learned:

1. Always change one jumper at a time.
2. Write down the old jumper position.
3. Do not do this before a school project is due.
4. The reset button is your friend.

If you have overclocked a 486 and have advice, sign my guestbook. If you have advice about not overclocking a 486, keep it to yourself.
