This is the third version of my homepage and I am finally happy with it.

The old one had a frameset, a Java applet that never loaded, and a background image of clouds. It took eleven seconds to load on a 56k modem and my friend said it looked like a screensaver. He was right.

The new site is built with tables, a tiled star background, and not one but **two** animated GIFs. I made the under-construction sign myself in Paint.

Here is what you can find:

- an about page with my machine specs
- a blog I promise to update
- a guestbook you should sign
- MIDI files, eventually

If anything looks broken, e-mail me. If the guestbook eats your message, that is because the CGI script is held together with hope.

Thanks for visiting. Bookmark me and come back soon.
